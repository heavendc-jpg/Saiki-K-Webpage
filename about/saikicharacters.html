The impact of foils, couples, and ironic character tropes in comedy animes


Who is Saiki Kusuo ?

Saiki Kusuo is the pink-haired protagonist psychic highschooler. He directly addresses the audience, often breaking the fourth wall. His blunt, dry humor often contrasts well with the disasterous and bizarre circumstances he finds himself in. He is ofen headcannoned as a 'tsundere' whose unreliable narration distorts the audience's perception of how he truly feels for his friends, who he goes the extra mile for to help with his psychic abilities.




Who is Teruhashi Kokomi ?

Teruhashi Kokomi is canonically the perfect pretty girl. She studies up late to memorize everyone's name, de-escalate conflicts with her beauty, and is aware of how she is percieved. As God's favorite, she attracts almost everybody, except for Saiki. Saiki K. often deliberately avoids her due to her nature to attract crowds, but also goes out of his way to make her life easier. Her narcissistic thoughts, revealed with Saiki's telepathy, become a topic of controversy on whether she is really 'perfect.' Unlike in the anime, her character often varies in likability among the fandom.
